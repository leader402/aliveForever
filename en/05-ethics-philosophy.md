## 🧠 Identity & Consciousness

### The Identity Paradox of Mind Uploading

| Question | Core Dilemma | Main Positions |
|---|---|---|
| **Ship of Theseus** | If neurons are gradually replaced by electronic components, is the result still "you"? | Gradualism vs. Essentialism |
| **Copying ≠ Transferring** | "Uploading" consciousness is actually "copying" — is the copy "you" while the original still exists? | Branch theory vs. Uniqueness theory |
| **Chinese Room** | Is functional equivalence (same behavior) the same as phenomenal equivalence (same experience)? | Functionalism vs. Biological naturalism |
| **Substrate of consciousness** | Does consciousness depend on carbon-based biology? Can silicon generate subjective experience? | Substrate independence vs. Carbon-exclusivity |

### Major Philosophical Positions

| Position | Key Thinker | Core Argument |
|---|---|---|
| **Functionalism** | Daniel Dennett, David Chalmers | Consciousness is determined by information processing patterns, substrate-independent |
| **Biological Naturalism** | John Searle | Consciousness is a product of biological processes; silicon simulation cannot produce true consciousness |
| **Integrated Information Theory (IIT)** | Giulio Tononi | Consciousness depends on degree of information integration (Φ); some artificial systems could be conscious |
| **Panpsychism** | Philip Goff | Consciousness is a fundamental property of matter, not limited to biological organisms |

## 🌍 Societal Impact

### Equity & Inequality

| Issue | Core Concern |
|---|---|
| **Rich-only access?** | Will expensive technologies (stem cells, gene therapy, cryonics) serve only the ultra-wealthy? |
| **Lifespan divide** | Will life-extension tech widen existing wealth-longevity gaps? |
| **Generational ossification** | If power-holders "never die," will social classes become permanently fixed? |
| **Resource competition** | Extreme longevity population pressure on Earth's resources, jobs, housing |

### Population & Resources

- If human lifespan doubles, can Earth's carrying capacity sustain it?
- Retirement systems and pension structures face fundamental restructuring
- Fertility-longevity balance: does a long-lived society require lower birth rates?

### The Meaning of Death

- Does death give life its meaning? Would life without death lose urgency and preciousness?
- Philosopher Bernard Williams: "Immortality would be intolerably boring"
- Counter-argument: Longer life means more learning, creation, and experience

## ⚗️ Scientific Ethics

### Animal Experimentation

| Issue | Status |
|---|---|
| Neuralink animal experiments | Alleged ~1,500 animal deaths over 4 years, under federal investigation — [Reuters Investigation](https://www.reuters.com/technology/neuralink-staff-cite-animal-testing-problems-elon-musks-brain-chip-company-2022-12-05/) |
| Aging research mouse experiments | Extensive use of genetically engineered mice, involving long-term suffering (e.g., progeroid models) |
| Parabiosis experiments | Surgically joining young-old mice raises animal welfare concerns |

### Unvalidated Commercialization

| Issue | Case |
|---|---|
| Stem cell tourism | Wealthy individuals traveling to Ukraine for embryonic stem cell injections (~$90K/shot), scientifically unvalidated |
| NMN/NR hype | Some brands exaggerate anti-aging effects; actual human clinical evidence insufficient |
| Young plasma commercialization | Ambrosia and others sold young plasma infusions; FDA warned against them |

### Cryopreservation Ethics

- 🔴 Preserved individuals are legally dead — if revived, how to establish identity?
- 🔴 If only the brain is preserved, where does the "new body" come from? Clone? Artificial? What are the ethics?
- 🔴 Are cryopreservation contracts promises that may never be fulfilled?
