## 📌 Key Knowledge Framework

### The Twelve Hallmarks of Aging

The 2023 Cell review expanded the hallmarks from 9 to 12:

| # | Hallmark | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Genomic instability | Accumulation of DNA damage with age |
| 2 | Telomere attrition | Telomere shortening leading to replicative senescence |
| 3 | Epigenetic alterations | DNA methylation and other modifications drift with age |
| 4 | Loss of proteostasis | Increased protein misfolding and aggregation |
| 5 | Disabled macroautophagy | Decline in cellular "self-cleaning" capacity |
| 6 | Deregulated nutrient-sensing | Changes in mTOR / AMPK / Sirtuins pathways |
| 7 | Mitochondrial dysfunction | Decreased energy metabolism efficiency, increased ROS |
| 8 | Cellular senescence | Accumulation of senescent cells, SASP factor release |
| 9 | Stem cell exhaustion | Decline in tissue regenerative capacity |
| 10 | Altered intercellular communication | Chronic inflammation (inflammaging), etc. |
| 11 | Chronic inflammation | Persistent systemic low-grade inflammation |
| 12 | Dysbiosis | Age-related changes in microbiome composition |

## 🔬 Key Research Tracking

### Genetics & Molecular Pathways

| Date | Discovery | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| Classic | C. elegans DAF-2 mutation (insulin/IGF-1 pathway inhibition) extends lifespan 5x | 🟢 | Nature series |
| Classic | FOXO3 gene polymorphisms associated with human centenarian longevity | 🟢 | Multiple independent population studies |
| Classic | APOE alleles linked to longevity and Alzheimer's risk | 🟢 | Large-scale GWAS |
| Classic | mTOR pathway inhibition mimics caloric restriction lifespan extension (multi-species) | 🟢 | Yeast → worms → flies → mice |
| Classic | Rapamycin extends mouse lifespan by 10-20% | 🟢 | NIA ITP multi-center validation |
| Classic | SIRT6 overexpression extends male mouse lifespan ~15% | 🟡 | Single study, sex-dependent |

### Epigenetics & Reprogramming

| Date | Discovery | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| Classic | Epigenetic clocks (Horvath Clock) accurately predict biological age | 🟢 | Multiple validations |
| 2016 | Yamanaka factors (OSKM) partial reprogramming reverses aging in progeroid mice | 🟡 | Salk Institute, Cell |
| Ongoing | Caloric restriction slows epigenetic clock rate | 🟢 | CALERIE human trial et al. |

### Telomeres & Cellular Senescence

| Date | Discovery | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| Classic | Telomere shortening is a core driver of replicative senescence | 🟢 | Nobel Prize 2009 |
| Classic | TERC/TERT knockout mice show accelerated aging | 🟢 | Multiple labs replicated |
| Classic | Telomerase induction in adult mice reverses progeroid phenotype | 🟡 | Specific model, safety TBD |
| Ongoing | Senolytics delay multiple age-related diseases in mice | 🟡 | Transgenic mice → early human trials |
