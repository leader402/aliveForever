## 📌 Key Knowledge Framework

### The Twelve Hallmarks of Aging

The 2023 Cell review expanded the hallmarks from 9 to 12:

| # | Hallmark | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Genomic instability | Accumulation of DNA damage with age |
| 2 | Telomere attrition | Telomere shortening leading to replicative senescence |
| 3 | Epigenetic alterations | DNA methylation and other modifications drift with age |
| 4 | Loss of proteostasis | Increased protein misfolding and aggregation |
| 5 | Disabled macroautophagy | Decline in cellular "self-cleaning" capacity |
| 6 | Deregulated nutrient-sensing | Changes in mTOR / AMPK / Sirtuins pathways |
| 7 | Mitochondrial dysfunction | Decreased energy metabolism efficiency, increased ROS |
| 8 | Cellular senescence | Accumulation of senescent cells, SASP factor release |
| 9 | Stem cell exhaustion | Decline in tissue regenerative capacity |
| 10 | Altered intercellular communication | Chronic inflammation (inflammaging), etc. |
| 11 | Chronic inflammation | Persistent systemic low-grade inflammation |
| 12 | Dysbiosis | Age-related changes in microbiome composition |

## 🔬 Key Research Tracking

### Genetics & Molecular Pathways

| Date | Discovery | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| Classic | C. elegans DAF-2 mutation (insulin/IGF-1 pathway inhibition) extends lifespan 5x | 🟢 | [Kenyon et al. 1993, *Nature*](https://doi.org/10.1038/366461a0) |
| Classic | FOXO3 gene polymorphisms associated with human centenarian longevity | 🟢 | [Willcox et al. 2008, *PNAS*](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0801030105) |
| Classic | APOE alleles linked to longevity and Alzheimer's risk | 🟢 | [Deelen et al. 2019, *Nat Commun*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-11558-2) |
| Classic | mTOR pathway inhibition mimics caloric restriction lifespan extension (multi-species) | 🟢 | [Harrison et al. 2009, *Nature*](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08221) |
| Classic | Rapamycin extends mouse lifespan by 10-20% | 🟢 | [Harrison et al. 2009, *Nature* (NIA ITP)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08221) |
| Classic | SIRT6 overexpression extends male mouse lifespan ~15% | 🟡 | [Kanfi et al. 2012, *Nature*](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature10815) |

### Epigenetics & Reprogramming

| Date | Discovery | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| Classic | Epigenetic clocks (Horvath Clock) accurately predict biological age | 🟢 | [Horvath 2013, *Genome Biol*](https://doi.org/10.1186/gb-2013-14-10-r115) |
| 2016 | Yamanaka factors (OSKM) partial reprogramming reverses aging in progeroid mice | 🟡 | [Ocampo et al. 2016, *Cell*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2016.11.052) |
| Ongoing | Caloric restriction slows epigenetic clock rate | 🟢 | [Waziry et al. 2023, *Nat Aging*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-022-00357-y) |

### Telomeres & Cellular Senescence

| Date | Discovery | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| Classic | Telomere shortening is a core driver of replicative senescence | 🟢 | [2009 Nobel Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/medicine/2009/summary/) |
| Classic | TERC/TERT knockout mice show accelerated aging | 🟢 | [Blasco et al. 1997, *Cell*](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0092-8674(00)81846-0) |
| Classic | Telomerase induction in adult mice reverses progeroid phenotype | 🟡 | [Jaskelioff et al. 2011, *Nature*](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature09603) |
| Ongoing | Senolytics delay multiple age-related diseases in mice | 🟡 | [Baker et al. 2016, *Nature*](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature16932) |
