## �📑 Knowledge Base Sections

### 🧬 [Longevity Research Frontiers](01-longevity-research.md)
Fundamental aging research: genes & molecular pathways, epigenetic clocks, telomeres & telomerase, cellular senescence, senolytics, hallmarks of aging, model organism lifespan studies.

### 💊 [Interventions & Practice](02-interventions-practice.md)
Known or potential life-extension methods: caloric restriction & intermittent fasting, exercise protocols, NAD+ precursors (NMN/NR), rapamycin, metformin, stem cell therapy, young blood/parabiosis, supplements, biohacking.

### 🧠 [Consciousness & Immortality Tech](03-consciousness-immortality.md)
Digital consciousness frontiers: whole brain emulation (WBE), brain-computer interfaces (BCI/Neuralink), connectomics, cryopreservation, digital mind copying, AI personality simulation.

### 🏢 [Industry & Capital](04-industry-capital.md)
Longevity & consciousness immortality business ecosystem: key companies (Altos Labs, Calico, Unity Biotech, etc.), funding events, clinical trial progress, regulatory developments.

### ⚖️ [Ethics & Philosophy](05-ethics-philosophy.md)
Deep questions raised by life extension & immortality: identity paradoxes of mind uploading, moral status of digital consciousness, social equity, population & resources, religious & cultural perspectives.

## 📊 Evidence Level Legend

Every entry is tagged with one of these evidence levels:

| Tag | Level | Meaning | Typical Sources |
|:---:|:---:|---|---|
| 🟢 | **High** | Peer-reviewed & independently replicated | Nature / Science / Cell papers, large RCTs, systematic reviews |
| 🟡 | **Medium** | Preliminary evidence, needs further validation | Preprints, animal models, small human trials, institutional reports |
| 🔴 | **Low** | Speculative or very early stage | News reports, expert opinions, proof-of-concept, industry rumors |
