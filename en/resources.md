## 📄 Landmark Papers

### 🧬 Longevity & Aging

| Paper | Journal | Year | Significance |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| López-Otín et al. — *Hallmarks of Aging* | Cell | 2013 | Defined the 9 original hallmarks framework |
| López-Otín et al. — *Hallmarks of Aging: An Expanding Universe* | Cell | 2023 | Updated to 12 hallmarks with new discoveries |
| Horvath — *DNA methylation age of human tissues and cell types* | Genome Biology | 2013 | Pioneering work on epigenetic clocks |
| Yamanaka — *Induction of Pluripotent Stem Cells* | Cell | 2006 | Foundation of cellular reprogramming (Nobel Prize) |
| Conboy et al. — *Rejuvenation of aged progenitor cells...* | Nature | 2005 | Parabiosis experiments — origin of young blood research |
| Kaeberlein et al. — *Rapamycin extends lifespan in mice* | Nature | 2009 | Key evidence for mTOR inhibition in lifespan extension |
| TAME Trial Protocol — *Targeting Aging with Metformin* | Cell Metabolism | 2016 | First large RCT design with "aging" as the indication |

### 🧠 Consciousness & Digital Immortality

| Paper | Journal | Year | Significance |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| Sandberg & Bostrom — *Whole Brain Emulation: A Roadmap* | FHI Technical Report | 2008 | WBE technical roadmap, still the reference baseline |
| Markram — *The Blue Brain Project* | Nature Reviews Neuroscience | 2006 | First systematic large-scale brain simulation attempt |
| Neuralink — *An integrated brain-machine interface platform* | bioRxiv | 2019 | Neuralink core technology white paper |
| Tononi — *Integrated Information Theory (IIT) 3.0* | PLOS Comp. Biology | 2014 | Theoretical framework for quantifying consciousness |
| Koch & Tononi — *Can Machines Be Conscious?* | IEEE Spectrum | 2023 | Frontier discussion on machine consciousness |
