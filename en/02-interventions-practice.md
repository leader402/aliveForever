## 🥗 Dietary Interventions

### Caloric Restriction (CR)

**Key Findings:**
- 🟢 First discovered in 1935 (rats); validated across yeast → worms → flies → mice → primates — [McCay et al. 1935](https://doi.org/10.1093/jn/10.1.63)
- 🟢 CR improves weight, glucose regulation, blood pressure, inflammation
- 🟡 Wisconsin rhesus monkey study: 30% CR extended lifespan, reduced age-related disease — [Colman et al. 2009, *Science*](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1173635)
- 🟡 NIA monkey study: health improved but lifespan extension not significant (experimental conditions differ) — [Mattison et al. 2012, *Nature*](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature11432)
- 🟡 Human CALERIE trial: 25% CR for 2 years slowed aging rate by ~2-3% — [Belsky et al. 2017, *J Gerontol*](https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/glx082)

### Intermittent Fasting & Meal Timing

**Key Findings:**
- 🟡 IF can extend mouse lifespan, but typically less effective than sustained CR
- 🟡 Simply changing eating patterns without reducing total calories — no lifespan extension in some mice
- 🟢 **Meal timing is critical**: Aligning restricted calories with active phase (circadian rhythm) extended mouse lifespan by **35%** total — [Acosta-Rodríguez et al. 2022, *Science*](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abk0297)

## 🏃 Exercise

**Key Findings:**
- 🟢 Regular exercise increases life expectancy by 0.4-6.9 years — [Moore et al. 2012, *PLoS Med*](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1001335)
- 🟢 30 min/day moderate exercise reduces all-cause mortality by ~30%
- 🟢 Elderly: 150 min/week moderate aerobic exercise reduces all-cause mortality by 31% — [Arem et al. 2015, *JAMA Intern Med*](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamainternmed.2015.0533)
- 🟢 Broad benefits: cardiovascular, metabolic, musculoskeletal, cognitive
- 🟡 Excessive exercise may have negative effects — "moderate and consistent" is key

## 💊 Drugs & Supplements

### NMN / NR (NAD+ Precursors)

| Dimension | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|:---:|
| Animal studies | NMN improves insulin sensitivity, DNA repair, muscle endurance in aged mice | 🟡 |
| Animal lifespan | Lifelong NMN extends mouse lifespan ~8-10% | 🟡 |
| Human trials | Systematic analysis of 25 NR trials: no significant anti-aging clinical benefit | 🟢 (negative) |
| Regulatory | FDA removed NMN from dietary supplement list | — |

### Rapamycin

| Dimension | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|:---:|
| Animal studies | NIA ITP multi-center: extends mouse lifespan 10-20% | 🟢 |
| Mechanism | mTOR inhibition, mimics caloric restriction | 🟢 |
| Human trials | Low-dose rapamycin anti-aging trials ongoing | 🟡 |
| Side effects | Immunosuppression, dyslipidemia (long-term low-dose risk TBD) | ⚠️ |

## 🩸 Emerging Therapies

### Young Blood Plasma / Parabiosis

- 🟡 2005 Conboy experiment: old-young mouse parabiosis enhanced tissue regeneration — [Conboy et al. 2005, *Nature*](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature03260)
- 🟡 2023 Nature Aging: 3-month parabiosis extended old mouse lifespan ~10%, reduced epigenetic age — [Zhang et al. 2023, *Nat Aging*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-023-00451-9)
- 🟡 2020: Diluting old mouse plasma (saline + albumin) achieved similar rejuvenation — [Mehdipour et al. 2020, *Aging*](https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.103418)
- 🔴 Bryan Johnson's "tri-generational blood exchange": self-reported "no benefit" — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2023-01-25/anti-aging-techniques-high-tech-billionaire-bryan-johnson-is-trying)
- ⚠️ FDA issued safety warnings against "young plasma therapy" — [FDA Safety Communication](https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/safety-availability-biologics/statement-fda-commissioner-scott-gottlieb-md-and-director-fdas-center-biologics-evaluation-and-0)

### Stem Cell Therapy

- 🟡 MSC infusion improved frailty symptoms in elderly patients (double-blind RCT) — [Tompkins et al. 2017, *J Gerontol*](https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/glx056)
- 🔴 "Stem cell tourism" (e.g., Ukraine embryonic stem cell injections) lacks clinical validation
- ⚠️ Mainstream medicine remains cautious about stem cells for anti-aging
