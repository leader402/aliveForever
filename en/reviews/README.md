## 📐 What's In Each Review?

Every semi-annual review contains:

### 1. 🏆 Top 10 Breakthroughs
The most impactful findings or events of the half-year

### 2. 📈 Confidence Level Upgrades & Downgrades
Reviewing past entries' evidence levels:
- ⬆️ **Upgraded**: 🔴→🟡 or 🟡→🟢 (new supporting evidence)
- ⬇️ **Downgraded**: 🟢→🟡 or 🟡→🔴 (challenged or refuted)
- ➡️ **Maintained**: Evidence level unchanged

### 3. 🔬 Trend Analysis by Field
Trend summary for each of the 5 sections

### 4. 💡 Emerging Directions
Brand new research directions or concepts that emerged

### 5. ⚠️ Challenged or Refuted Findings
Research that was once promising but later questioned

### 6. 🔮 Outlook for Next Half-Year
Upcoming papers, trial results, and industry events to watch
