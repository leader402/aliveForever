## 🖥️ Whole Brain Emulation (WBE)

**Concept:** Scan a specific brain at sub-cellular resolution, build an identical digital neural network model on a computer, and run it to reproduce all brain functions — including conscious experience.

**Key Milestones:**

| Date | Event | Evidence |
|---|---|:---:|
| 1988 | Hans Moravec describes "gradual consciousness transfer" thought experiment | — |
| 2005 | Blue Brain Project launched — simulating rat neocortical column | 🟡 |
| 2014 | OpenWorm project simulates C. elegans complete connectome (302 neurons) | 🟡 |
| 2019 | Complete fruit fly brain connectome (~100K neurons) published | 🟢 |
| 2024 | First 1 mm³ human brain tissue synapse-level connectome (Google + Harvard) | 🟢 |
| Future | Complete human brain connectome (~86B neurons, ~150T synapses) → long-term goal | 🔴 |

**Core Challenges:**
- ⚡ **Scanning resolution**: Requires nanometer-level whole-brain 3D imaging
- 💾 **Data volume**: Complete human connectome estimated at ZB (Zettabyte) scale
- 🖥️ **Compute power**: Simulating 86B neurons in real-time requires far beyond current capability
- ❓ **Consciousness mechanism**: We don't fully understand how consciousness emerges from neural activity

## 🔌 Brain-Computer Interface (BCI)

**Concept:** Establish direct communication between brain and computers — seen as a "bridge between human brain and machine," potentially an intermediate step toward consciousness immortality.

### Neuralink Timeline

| Date | Event | Evidence |
|---|---|:---:|

| 2019 | Musk claims monkeys can control computers via implanted interface | 🔴 |
| 2020.08 | Demo of pig "Gertrude" with implanted chip recording olfactory signals | 🟡 |
| 2021.04 | Monkey "Pager" plays Pong via brain chip — mind-controlled | 🟡 |
| 2022.12 | Monkey "Sake" achieves mind-typing, inputs complete sentences | 🟡 |
| 2023 | FDA approves Neuralink's first human clinical trial | 🟢 |

## 🧊 Cryopreservation (Cryonics)

**Concept:** After legal death, vitrification at -196°C in liquid nitrogen preserves the body or brain, hoping future technology can enable revival.

**Current Data (Alcor, as of Feb 2025):**
- Preserved "patients": **248**
- Registered members: **1,442**
- Whole-body cost: ~$200,000
- Brain-only (neuropreservation): ~$80,000

**Key Facts:**

| Item | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|:---:|
| Mammalian revival from cryopreservation | **Zero** cases | 🟢 (confirmed negative) |
| Small organisms (C. elegans, etc.) frozen & revived | Achieved | 🟢 |
| Vitrified rabbit kidney restored to function (2009) | Achieved | 🟡 |
| Pig brain synaptic structure preserved intact (2018) | Achieved | 🟡 |
| Nectome: chemical fixation + cryo for brain connectome preservation | In research | 🔴 |

## 💾 Digital Mind Copying & AI Personality Simulation

**Core Issues with Digital Copying:**
- "Uploading" is actually "copying," not "transferring" — two "selves" would coexist
- Does the digital copy possess subjective experience (does it "feel")?
- Is functional equivalence (same behavior) the same as phenomenal equivalence (same experience)?

**AI Personality Simulation Status:**
- 🔴 AI twins trained on personal text/audio/video data already have commercial products
- 🔴 Can only mimic surface behavior, cannot replicate inner consciousness
- 🔴 Closer to "digital legacy/memorial" than true consciousness continuation
